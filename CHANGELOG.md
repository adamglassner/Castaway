# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-12

First preview of Castaway for Windows: the player app's shell.

- Sidebar with Home, the six libraries (Movies, TV Shows, Kids & Family, Documentaries, Stand-Up, Anime),
  and Castaway Server, Remote Access and Activity.
- Home preview built from the Castaway mockup, with made-up sample titles: Continue Watching,
  Recently Added Movies and Recently Added TV.
- Every other section is a "coming soon" page that describes what it will do and lists its planned features.
- Light and dark mode (sidebar toggle, or Settings → Appearance, including "Match Windows").
- Installer and portable zip, installable and updatable from the Bytehawk Studio Launcher.
